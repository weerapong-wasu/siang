The 44 Consonants

Every Thai consonant belongs to one of three classes — Middle, High, or Low. The class decides how tones work later. Tap any card to hear it and see details.

Vowels — Short & Long

All 32 traditional Thai vowels (สระ ๓๒): 12 short/long pairs + 8 special forms. Length changes meaning! The อ shows where the consonant sits. Tap to hear the difference.

The 5 Tones

The same syllable with a different pitch = a completely different word. Watch the pitch line, then tap to hear each tone.

🧪 Minimal-Pair Lab

Famous word sets that differ only by tone. This is where foreigners laugh — and learn.

Survival Thai 🧳

The phrases foreigners actually need — tap any line to hear it. Phonetics show tones with marks: à low · â falling · á high · ǎ rising · plain = mid.
